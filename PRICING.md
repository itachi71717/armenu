# AR Menu — Pricing Structure

**Document version:** 1.0 · **Date:** April 2026 · **Internal — do not show clients verbatim. Use as your reference; price in person.**

---

## Pricing philosophy

**You are not selling software. You are selling a service that includes software.**

The 3D scanning, calibration, model cleanup, QR design, monthly reporting — that's the work. The platform is the multiplier. Price like a boutique production agency, not a SaaS app.

Three principles:
1. **Setup fee captures the production cost.** One-time, non-refundable, prepaid.
2. **Monthly retainer captures the platform value.** Locks in recurring revenue, justifies hosting + reports + support.
3. **Per-dish add-on captures expansion.** Restaurants that grow their menu pay per addition.

**Never undercut to win.** Photo menus cost R0; if you compete on price, you lose. Compete on irreplaceability.

## Pricing posture (how you talk about it)

When a restaurant asks "how much does this cost":

> "It depends on how many of your dishes you'd want in AR. Most restaurants we work with land on between R1,800 and R3,500 a month, plus a once-off setup fee of around R8,500 to scan your hero dishes and deliver everything ready to go. We'd want to come and see your menu before quoting properly — can I drop by Tuesday?"

**Anchors them at the right number, doesn't commit, books a meeting.** Never quote on the phone or in DMs.

## Negotiation rules

| Situation | Response |
|---|---|
| "Can I just pay monthly with no setup fee?" | No. Setup covers production cost we already bore. We can split setup over 3 months at +10%. |
| "What if I scan my own dishes?" | Available in V2. Until then, scanning is part of the service. |
| "Can we go month-to-month?" | Restaurant tier is 12 months. Starter is 6. The Cape Town pilot got founder-special terms; they're not on offer anymore. |
| "Spur down the road only paid X" | "Different scope. Spur is on Chain pricing with 20 locations. Apples to oranges." |
| "We don't have budget right now" | "Understood. The Starter tier is R450/month — gets you one hero dish. Most restaurants upgrade in 60 days. Let's start there." |
| "Free trial?" | No free trials. First month at 50% off in exchange for a video testimonial — only for the first 5 clients ever, and that window is closing. |

## Pricing review cadence

- **Every 6 months:** review &amp; raise prices for new clients only (existing clients are grandfathered)
- **Annual:** raise existing-client monthly fees by 8% (CPI + value capture)
- **At 50 clients:** raise Restaurant tier setup to R12,000 and monthly to R2,500. Justified by demand &amp; brand strength.
- **At 100 clients:** add an Enterprise tier with custom pricing
