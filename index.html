FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does the diner need to download an app?

No. The QR opens in their phone's native camera and loads our AR page in any browser. Zero installs, zero accounts, zero friction.

Will it work on every phone?

Every iPhone from 2017 onwards and almost every Android from 2018 onwards. Older devices still see a 3D model they can rotate with their finger — nobody hits a broken page.

How accurate is the portion size?

Each dish is captured with a calibrated reference object so the AR model appears at exact real-world size. What the diner sees is what they get.

How long does setup take?

About one week from signing. We come to your kitchen, capture your top dishes, deliver printed QR codes, and you're live.

Can I add new dishes later?

Yes. We handle the scanning, modelling, and QR generation end-to-end. Pricing for additional dishes is discussed in person.

How do I know it's working?

You receive a private monthly performance report with scan counts per dish, peak times, and trends — designed to guide your menu decisions.
