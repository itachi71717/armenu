How it works

Quick scan. Zero friction.

The guest opens their phone's native camera, points at the QR, and the whole experience is live. Nothing to install.

1

Scan the QR

Guest points their phone's native camera at the table QR — the menu opens directly through the camera, no separate app required.

2

Menu opens instantly

Your branded menu — logo, colours, cover — loads in under 2 seconds on a global edge CDN.

3

Tap → see it in AR

Guest taps a dish. iOS Quick Look and Android Scene Viewer open — the dish appears life-size on their table.

4

Order & review

If you want it, guests can order from the menu and one-tap review after the meal. Both are optional.

Built-in features

Every feature your restaurant needs. Already included.

The AR menu is always on. The order cart, waiter call and kitchen display are optional modules — turn them on only if you don't already have a POS you love.

4D Dishes

Tap → life-size on the table via iOS Quick Look / Android Scene Viewer. Real WebAR, no gimmick.

Real-time menu

Edit a price, mark a dish sold out, swap a photo — live on every guest's screen within seconds.

Multi-menu scheduler

Breakfast → lunch → dinner switches automatically on the clock you set.

Multi-language & currency

Up to 8 languages, 8 currencies. Guests pick what suits them and the menu adapts instantly.

Auto happy hour

Schedule discounts on the days and hours you choose. Happy hour, brunch specials, late-night deals.

Custom branding

Your logo, your colours, your cover image — the menu looks unmistakably like your restaurant.

Google Reviews routing

4-5★ prompt Google review; 1-3★ stays private for you. Growth without astroturfing.

AI Menu Reader

Upload a PDF or photo of your current menu. Claude Vision extracts every dish + category in one click.

AI Dish Generator

One photo per dish → a 3D .glb model in 1-3 minutes via Meshy AI. No 3D-scanning expertise needed.

Order cart & kitchen

Guests build a cart, kitchen sees the order live. Keep your existing POS? Just leave this off.

Waiter call button

1-tap on the guest menu → alert on the staff console. Off by default.

Table + staff console

Per-table QRs pin orders to seats. Waiters sign in with PIN → live orders + table board.
