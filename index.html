Questions

Anything you'd want to ask.

Do guests need to download an app?

Never. The whole experience runs through the phone's native camera and browser. AR uses iOS Quick Look and Android Scene Viewer, both installed on every modern phone by default.

How long does setup take?

An afternoon. We onboard you personally so you go live before your next dinner service.

Do I have to use your ordering system?

No. The AR menu is the core. Order cart, waiter call and kitchen display are optional. Leave them off if you already have a POS you love.

How do the 3D dish models get made?

Upload a well lit photo to AR Studio. The platform generates a life size 3D model in a few minutes. Not happy with it? Retry with one tap. Adjust the size before you publish.

What does it cost?

Pricing is per restaurant. Get in touch and we'll build a proposal for you.

Do you support Afrikaans, Zulu, Xhosa?

Yes. Up to 8 languages per menu with per dish translations. Guests pick their language and the menu adapts.
