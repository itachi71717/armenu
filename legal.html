2. Privacy Policy (POPI Act + GDPR)

This section explains what personal information we collect, why, how long we hold it, and the rights you have under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPI Act") in South Africa and the General Data Protection Regulation (EU 2016/679, "GDPR") where a data subject is in the EU. ARMenu is the "responsible party" under POPI (equivalent to "data controller" under GDPR).

ARMenu (armenu.co.za) is the data controller for personal information it collects directly to operate the platform (manager, staff and billing accounts). For personal information a restaurant collects about its own diners through ARMenu (orders, reservations, reviews), the restaurant is the data controller and ARMenu acts as processor on the restaurant's instructions — see section 5.

2.1 What we collect

Restaurant managers & owners:

  • Email address (required — used as the login identifier).
  • Display name (as returned by your Clerk profile).
  • Business name, address, WhatsApp phone (optional — if you enter them).
  • Timestamp and IP address of each sign-in.

Staff and kitchen users:

  • Display name.
  • A SHA-256 hash of the 4–6 digit PIN salted with the restaurant identifier (we cannot read your PIN — we can only verify it).
  • Last-login timestamp.

Diners:

  • No profile is created. No email or password.
  • Scan events are logged with a hashed IP fragment, browser user-agent, country, and optional table label — used only for aggregate analytics reported to the restaurant.
  • Orders you place are stored against the restaurant that received them, with a table label (if provided) and any note you typed.
  • Table reservations include the name and phone number you submit.
  • Reviews you leave are stored with a rating, optional text, and a routing decision (4–5 stars are offered a Google review link; 1–3 stars stay private for the restaurant).

2.2 Why we process it (lawful basis)

  • Performance of contract — accounts, subscriptions, orders and reservations are processed to deliver the service you asked for.
  • Legitimate interests — aggregate scan and order analytics help us and the restaurant improve the menu and diagnose bugs.
  • Consent — where we ask for browser notifications, cookies beyond the essentials, or marketing communications, we ask you first and you can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation — invoicing and tax records are retained for the period required by South African tax law.

2.3 Where your data lives

  • Database (Neon Postgres, EU-central-1). Menu records, staff PINs (hashed), orders, reviews, reservations.
  • Media (Cloudflare R2, Western Europe). Restaurant logos, cover photos, dish 3D models (.glb / .usdz) and thumbnails.
  • Manager authentication (Clerk). Email address and OAuth tokens for magic-link and Google sign-in.
  • Payments (PayFast or equivalent). Card details are entered on and stored by the payment gateway, never on ARMenu servers.
  • Hosting (Vercel). Static files and serverless functions.
  • Optional third-party processors: Meshy (AI 3D generation from a photo), Anthropic Claude (AI menu import and translation) — called only when you or the restaurant manager explicitly triggers those features.

Cross-border transfers to the EU (Neon, R2, Clerk) and USA (Vercel, Anthropic) are covered by the recipient's Standard Contractual Clauses. We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

2.4 How long we keep it

  • Manager accounts & restaurant records: for the life of the subscription plus 90 days after cancellation, then hard-deleted.
  • Orders and reviews: 5 years for accounting and dispute resolution, then anonymised.
  • Reservations: 12 months.
  • Scan analytics: raw events 90 days; aggregated counts indefinitely.
  • Invoices and tax records: 5 years (South African Tax Administration Act).
  • Browser Push subscriptions: deleted the first time the browser signals the subscription is dead.

2.5 Your rights

You may exercise any of the following free of charge:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data.
  • Correction — update anything that is wrong or out of date.
  • Deletion — request permanent deletion (subject to legal retention).
  • Restriction / objection — pause or object to specific processing.
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent — for any consent-based processing.
  • Lodge a complaint — with the South African Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za) or your local EU supervisory authority.

Send requests to privacy@armenu.co.za. We respond within 30 days.

2.6 Security

  • All connections are HTTPS (TLS 1.3).
  • Staff PINs are hashed with SHA-256 + restaurant salt.
  • Manager tokens and admin tokens are HMAC-signed and short-lived (12 hours).
  • Every /api/admin/* request is scope-checked so a manager cannot access another restaurant's data.
  • Access to production databases is limited to authorised engineers using unique credentials.

2.7 Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided us data, email privacy@armenu.co.za and we will delete it.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

ARMenu uses browser storage sparingly. Everything below is either essential to the service or requires your consent.

Essential (always on)

  • Session cookies for manager sign-in (Clerk).
  • Local storage for the diner shopping cart, so it survives a page refresh.
  • Local storage for kitchen and staff PIN tokens, so tablets don't sign out mid-shift.
  • Local storage for the cookie-consent choice itself.

Optional (with your consent)

  • Web Push subscription — only after you tap "Turn on" on the opt-in banner.
  • Anonymised error reports (Sentry) — captures the error, page URL, and browser; strips personal fields.

No cross-site advertising cookies. No third-party marketing pixels.
