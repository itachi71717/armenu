The basics

Common questions.

Does the diner need to download an app?

No. The QR opens in their phone's native camera and loads our AR page in any browser. Zero installs, zero accounts, zero friction. It's a link. Diners already know how to follow a link.

Will it work on every phone?

Every iPhone from 2017 onwards (running iOS 12+) and almost every Android from 2018 onwards. Older devices still see a fully interactive 3D model they can rotate with their finger. nobody hits a broken page. Safari on iPhone delivers full AR Quick Look; Chrome on Android uses Scene Viewer or WebXR.

How accurate is the portion size?

Each dish is captured with a calibrated reference object so the AR model appears at exact real-world size. What the diner sees is what they get. down to the centimetre. This is deliberately the hardest part of the service to fake, and the part we're proudest of.

How long does setup take?

About one week from signing. We schedule a kitchen session, capture your top dishes using our scanning rig, process the models to AR-ready quality, deliver printed QR codes and collateral, and you're live. Restaurants are usually surprised at how smooth the handover is.

Can I add new dishes later?

Yes. the platform is designed to grow with your menu. We handle all new scanning, modelling, and QR generation end-to-end. Pricing for additional dishes after the pilot is discussed in person based on your volume.

How do I know it's working?

You receive a private monthly performance report showing scan counts per dish, peak times, day-of-week trends, and quarter-on-quarter comparisons. It's designed to guide real menu decisions. not just look pretty in a deck.

What if a dish changes appearance seasonally?

We re-scan. Keeping your AR models accurate to what actually arrives at the table is part of the service. We'll discuss a rescan schedule during onboarding that matches how often your menu or plating evolves.

Is there a contract or minimum term?

We currently onboard restaurants as pilot partners. Terms are discussed in person and structured to be fair for both sides. We'd rather earn your long-term business than lock you in.

What does it cost?

Pricing is per restaurant, not a fixed tier. Every kitchen is different. dish count, table count, languages, staff, and which optional modules you switch on. We build a proposal that fits. Get in touch and we'll walk you through what your setup looks like.

Do you actually come to our kitchen?

Yes. Our team visits on a booked date to scan your top dishes with the calibrated rig. one session usually covers a full menu. If you're outside Johannesburg, Cape Town, or Durban we travel to you. costs are folded into the proposal so there are no surprise line items.

What quality are the 3D dish files?

Each dish ships as a compressed GLB, typically 2-4 MB, tuned to render in under two seconds on 4G. Textures are 2K PBR, geometry is decimated for mobile GPUs, and every model is validated in iOS Quick Look and Android Scene Viewer before it goes live. The result feels photoreal at arm's length. exactly where a diner holds their phone.

How fast is WhatsApp support?

Business hours (Mon-Sat, 08:00-20:00 SAST) we reply on WhatsApp within roughly 30 minutes. usually much faster. Overnight and Sunday messages are answered by 09:00 the next morning. For anything that stops service, message us and we escalate immediately.

Still have questions?

We respond to every restaurant inquiry personally. usually within a few hours during business hours.
