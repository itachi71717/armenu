How it works

From curious
to ordered.

Three steps. Two seconds. No app required.

Scroll
01 · Scan i

Point. Scan.

Diner aims their camera at the QR printed beside the dish. No app. No account.

02 · Tap ii

One tap.

Our page loads in under a second. The 3D model is already rendering as it opens.

03 · See iii

Meal on table.

Life-size, photo-real, true portion. They walk around it. They decide. They order.

i

Works on every modern phone

Every iPhone from 2017 onwards and almost every Android from 2018. Older devices still show a 3D model the diner can rotate. nobody lands on a broken page.

ii

No app install required

The QR opens directly in the phone's native camera and loads the AR page in Safari or Chrome. Zero new software. Zero new accounts. Pure web.

iii

Photo-real, calibrated size

Each dish is captured with a reference object so the model appears at exact real-world portion size. What the diner sees is precisely what they get.

iv

We handle everything

We come to your kitchen, capture your dishes, process the models, set up the QR codes, and hand over printed collateral. You're live within a week.
