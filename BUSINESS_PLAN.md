# AR Menu — Business Plan

**Document version:** 1.0 · **Date:** April 2026 · **Owner:** Founder (you)

---

## 1. Executive summary

**AR Menu** is South Africa's first augmented-reality menu platform. Diners scan a QR code printed beside any dish on a restaurant menu and a photo-real, life-size 3D model of that meal appears on their table — no app, no download, no friction.

We sell the platform to restaurants on a setup-fee + monthly retainer model, handling the entire production pipeline (3D scanning, model preparation, QR generation, hosting, monthly reporting) so the restaurant can focus on serving food.

The South African market is **completely untapped**. There is no direct local competitor delivering a turnkey AR menu service. We have a 12–18 month window to define the category and lock in mindshare before saturation.

**Target:** 50 paying restaurants in 12 months · ~R75,000 / month recurring revenue + setup fees · path to R3M+ ARR by year 3.

---

## 2. The problem

| Diner pain | Restaurant pain |
|---|---|
| Photos on menus look nothing like the plate that arrives | Returns &amp; complaints due to photo / portion mismatch |
| Hard to gauge portion size from a photo | Lost upsells: diners default to "safe" cheap option |
| Description-only menus don't sell premium dishes | No way to make a menu memorable on social media |
| Indecision &amp; long ordering time at table | Lower table turnover, frustrated waiters |

**Photo menus are an obsolete medium.** They were invented before phones, before AR, and before diners trained themselves to expect rich, interactive product previews (Amazon, IKEA, Shopify). Every other vertical has moved on. Restaurants are the last holdout.

## 3. The product

### 3.1 What the diner experiences
1. Sees a small QR icon next to a dish on the printed menu
2. Opens phone camera, points at QR
3. Tap notification (~1s)
4. Our AR page loads (~500ms)
5. Tap **See my meal**
6. **The meal appears, life-size, on their table**
7. Walks around it, leans in, decides

End-to-end: ~2 seconds. No app. No login. Works on every phone made after 2017.

### 3.2 What the restaurant gets
- 3D scans of their hero dishes (we come on-site, ~1 hour per restaurant)
- Print-ready QR codes (vector PDF, scalable, with center logo) to add to physical menus
- A **Restaurant Portal** login to download QRs and request changes
- A monthly performance report (PDF, branded) emailed on the 1st: scan counts, top dish, peak hour, trend vs prior month
- Hosting, uptime, AR magic — all handled

### 3.3 What we keep on our side (the moat)
- The scanning rig + know-how
- The boss panel (only owner can see all restaurants)
- The model library + processing pipeline
- The brand &amp; the domain

Restaurants depend on us for **every new dish, every menu change, every report**. They can't "do it themselves" because they don't have the scanning workflow or the back-end. This is by design.

## 6. Competitive landscape

| Player | What they do | Our edge |
|---|---|---|
| **Photo menus** | Status quo for 99% of SA restaurants | We replace photos entirely with a measurably better experience |
| **iMenu / digital menu PDFs** | QR → static PDF | Static, flat, no AR, no portion truth — we are an order of magnitude richer |
| **Global AR-menu startups** (Kabaq, QReal, in EU/US) | Similar concept, mostly US/EU only | None operate in SA, none have local hands for scanning, none speak ZAR |
| **Restaurant POS platforms** (GAAP, Pilot) | Order &amp; payment systems | Adjacent; we partner not compete |

**The risk is not local competition. It's a US/EU player landing in SA.** Our defence: speed. Sign 30 restaurants before they finish their go-to-market deck. Lock category-defining domain &amp; brand. Be unmistakably South African.

## 7. Go-to-market

### 7.1 Pilot phase (months 1–3)
**Goal: 3 paying lighthouse clients, free or discounted to anchor case studies.**
- Walk into 30 restaurants in person · pitch to the owner face-to-face
- Free first month for the first 3 sign-ups (in exchange for a video testimonial &amp; right to use their brand in case studies)
- Scan their hero dishes on the spot · show them the AR demo on the founder's phone

### 7.2 Growth phase (months 4–12)
**Goal: 50 paying clients, R75k MRR.**
- Inbound: landing page (`armenu.co.za`) collects WhatsApp leads
- Outbound: referrals from pilot clients (offer R500 credit per successful intro)
- Press: pitch a tech/lifestyle story to TimesLive, Daily Maverick, Bizcommunity ("First AR menus in SA")
- Social: Instagram &amp; TikTok content showing the AR experience — diners filming themselves is free distribution
- Trade events: Hostex (April), Africa's Big 7 — pay for a small booth, collect leads in person

### 7.3 Scale phase (year 2)
**Goal: 200 clients, R450k MRR.**
- Hire 1 sales rep + 1 scanning operator
- Add chains: pursue Spur Group, Famous Brands enterprise deals
- Open Cape Town &amp; Durban scanning teams
- Self-service tier: lower-end pricing for restaurants who scan their own dishes (on a tablet we lend them)

## 8. Operating model

### 8.1 Production pipeline (per restaurant)
| Step | Time | Tooling |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-meeting brief | 30 min | WhatsApp + email |
| On-site scanning | 60–90 min | Polycam Pro + softbox + turntable |
| Model cleanup &amp; calibration | 30 min per dish | Polycam → .glb |
| Upload via Boss Panel | 5 min | Our own platform |
| QR PDFs printed &amp; delivered | next day | Local printer |
| Monthly report generation | automatic | Our own platform |

**Total founder time per new restaurant: ~4 hours.** At 50 restaurants in 12 months, this is ~200 hours = manageable solo.

### 8.2 Tech stack (chosen for $0 fixed cost)
- Hosting: Cloudflare Pages (free)
- Database: Cloudflare D1 (free under 5GB)
- File storage: Cloudflare R2 (free under 10GB)
- API: Cloudflare Workers (free under 100k req/day)
- Auth: Clerk (free under 10k users)
- Domain: Host Africa (~R100/year)
- Scanning app: Polycam Pro (~R200/month, only when actively onboarding)

**Year 1 infrastructure cost: ~R3,500 total.** All else is founder time.

## 10. Risks &amp; mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |

| 3D scan quality complaints | Medium | Standardised scanning SOP; calibration check before any restaurant goes live |
| Founder burnout | High | Hire scanning operator at 20 clients, not 50 |
