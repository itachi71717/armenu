# AR Menu — Sales Playbook

**For walk-ins, WhatsApp DMs, and demo meetings.** Use this verbatim until you've internalised it. Then make it yours.

## The 60-second cold pitch (use this every time)

> "Hi, I'm [name] — I run AR Menu, South Africa's first augmented-reality menu platform. We put a small QR code next to each of your hero dishes. When a diner scans it, the meal appears on their actual table — life-size, photo-real, no app. Restaurants we work with see fewer returns and a 30%+ lift on premium dishes because diners can finally see what they're paying for. **Can I show you a 30-second demo?**"

→ Pull out your phone, open the AR demo, place a meal on their table. **Don't talk during the demo.** Watch their face.

After the demo:
> "Want me to come back tomorrow with three of your dishes scanned?"

## Objection handling (memorise these)

### "We already have nice photos on our menu."
> "Photos are flat — they don't show portion size, they don't show what comes with the dish. Half your returns are 'it looked bigger in the photo'. We make that disappear. Also, when's the last time a customer Instagrammed your menu? After AR, every table is a content moment."

### "Our customers are older — they won't use this."
> "Two thirds of South Africans over 40 use WhatsApp daily. If they can scan a Wi-Fi QR, they can scan this. We did a soft test in Sea Point — average user age was 47. They figured it out in under 8 seconds."

### "How much?"
> "Depends on your menu size. Most restaurants like yours land between R1,800 and R3,500 a month, with a once-off setup of around R8,500. I'd rather see your menu in person before quoting properly. **Tuesday morning work?**"

### "We need to think about it."
> "Of course. While you think — can I scan one dish, free, no commitment? You see it working on your own table, you decide. Tuesday I'm in this area anyway."

### "Send me an email and I'll consider it."
> "I will. But let's lock 15 minutes in your diary before I leave so you actually see the demo — emails get buried, the demo is what closes this."

### "Can you give us a free trial?"
> "Free trials make AR feel disposable. What I can do — first month at 50% off in exchange for a 30-second video testimonial when you see results. That offer's open to the next two restaurants who say yes today."

### "Will it slow down my staff or my Wi-Fi?"
> "Zero impact on either. It runs on the diner's phone over their data. Your Wi-Fi never sees it. Staff don't touch it."

### "What if a customer's phone is too old?"
> "Older phones still see a 3D model they can rotate with their finger. Nobody hits a broken page. About 95% of phones in active use show the full AR-on-table experience."

## What to do once they say yes

1. Send WhatsApp invoice for setup fee (use Yoco / EFT)
2. Schedule a 90-minute on-site scanning slot within 7 days
3. Bring: your phone, lazy susan, white tablecloth or paper, softbox light, credit card (for scale calibration)
4. Scan each dish following the SOP (see below)
5. Same evening: upload via boss panel
6. Within 48 hours: deliver printed QR codes (we partner with a same-day local printer)
7. Day 30: send first monthly report (even if mostly empty)
8. Day 35: WhatsApp check-in asking what feedback they've heard from diners

## Scanning Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

**Goal: every dish scanned the same way, every time. No surprises.**

### Setup (10 min)
1. Plate is fully built as served (garnish, sauce, sides — exactly the version a customer would receive)
2. White paper or tablecloth under the plate
3. Soft, even light — natural daylight at a window if possible, otherwise the softbox at 45°
4. Place a clean credit card flat on the table, ~10cm from the plate (this is your scale calibration)
5. Open Polycam → "Object" mode → "Photogrammetry"

### Capture (5 min per dish)
1. Walk slowly around the plate, phone held about 25cm away, dish kept centered in frame
2. **Three full circles**: low (eye-level with table), middle (45°), high (looking down at 70°)
3. Capture should take ~90 seconds. Polycam auto-shoots; you walk steadily.
4. Tap "Process" → wait 2–4 minutes

### Calibrate &amp; clean (5 min)
1. In Polycam edit, tell it the credit card is **85.6mm** wide. This locks scale.
2. Crop the credit card and table edge out
3. Auto-clean (one button)
4. Export → glTF Binary (.glb)

### Upload (1 min)
1. AirDrop / Google Drive the .glb to your laptop
2. Boss panel → Add new dish → drop the file → fill name + price → publish
3. System spits out the QR PDF — print it

### Validate (1 min)
1. Open the AR page on your phone
2. Tap "See my meal" → place on a table
3. Measure with a ruler. Real plate vs AR plate should match within 5mm.
4. If yes → ship. If no → re-scan.
